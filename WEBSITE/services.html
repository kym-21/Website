Our Services

Five ways we help hospitals, schools, and organizations build rehabilitation and inclusion into how they work.

Training

Rehabilitation Services

Space Design

Consulting
01 · Training

Training

We build the skills of individuals and teams working in rehabilitation, across three levels, from frontline specialists to the leaders shaping systems around them.

Specialist Training

Focused, practical training for rehabilitation specialists to deepen clinical and therapeutic skills.

Practitioner Training

Practical training for practitioners delivering rehabilitation and inclusion support every day.

Leadership and Systems Training

Equipping leaders and administrators to design and sustain rehabilitation systems within their organizations.

02 · Rehabilitation Services

Rehabilitation Services

Direct, practical rehabilitation support delivered through partnership models, bringing occupational therapy expertise into hospitals and organizations that need it.

Partnering with organizations

We work alongside organizations to provide occupational therapy (OT) and broader rehabilitation support.

Hospital OT partnership model

A structured partnership model that embeds OT services within hospital settings.

03 · Space Design

Space Design

Thoughtfully designed physical environments that support therapeutic goals and sensory needs.

Therapeutic space design

Designing spaces built with purpose for rehabilitation and therapy sessions.

Sensory room design and setup

Complete design and setup of sensory rooms tailored to the needs of the people who will use them.

04 · Consulting

Consulting

Organizational consulting to help institutions build the departments, programs, and systems that make rehabilitation and inclusion sustainable.

OT department setup

Supporting hospitals and organizations to establish occupational therapy departments from the ground up.

Organizational capacity building

Strengthening internal capacity so organizations can sustain rehabilitation and inclusion work independently.

Vocational rehabilitation program design and implementation

Designing and implementing vocational rehabilitation programs suited to organizational context and beneficiary needs.

School inclusion program

Helping schools design and implement programs that support inclusive education.

Systems and documentation

Building the workflows, protocols, and documentation that keep rehabilitation and inclusion work consistent and sustainable.

05 · Programs

Programs In Development

Programs we deliver directly to beneficiaries, beyond our training and consulting work.

Vocational Rehabilitation Program

A program currently in development, to be delivered directly to beneficiaries, helping people build skills and pathways toward meaningful work. Details to be announced.
