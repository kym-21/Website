Selected Work

A closer look at the organizations and teams ZIRI has worked with across Africa.

Zambia

Little Assisi Special Needs School

Specialist and practitioner training for a school working with children with special needs.

What we did

Delivered an interactive training session for staff working daily with children with special needs, covering practical strategies they could apply immediately in the classroom.

"The session was informative, practical, and highly relevant to the work we do every day with children with special needs. It was engaging and interactive, allowing us to ask questions, share experiences, and learn from the expertise of the facilitator. The session inspired us to continue improving our skills and to work more collaboratively in supporting every child's growth and potential."

Kenya

Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology

A workshop for the Occupational Therapy Department connecting classroom teaching to real world practice.

What we do

We create workshops for OT departments that connect classroom teaching to real world practice, with an emphasis on real life participation, vocational preparation, and community integration.

"The workshop was highly interactive, practical, and relevant to occupational therapy education and practice. It provided valuable insights into applying OT beyond the clinical setting, with a strong focus on real life participation, vocational preparation, and community integration. The facilitator made the concepts accessible and engaging, leaving us with practical ideas that can be applied in our teaching and practice."

JKUAT Occupational Therapy Department, Kenya

Kenya

Daughters of Charity of St. Vincent de Paul

Building a rehabilitation department from the ground up.

What we did

Established an occupational therapy department, including designing a full therapeutic field and sensory integration space, implementing a vocational rehabilitation program, and building the systems needed to sustain the work.

Pediatric and Mental Health Occupational Therapy

Direct clinical work behind ZIRI's training and consulting.

Background

Over 4 years working directly with children and families across autism, ADHD, cerebral palsy, and related mental health conditions, a foundation that continues to shape ZIRI's training and consulting work.
