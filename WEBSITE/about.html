Our Mission & Vision

What guides our work

Mission

To strengthen rehabilitation and inclusion by training practitioners, designing therapeutic environments, and building the systems organizations need to deliver care sustainably.

Vision

A future where every hospital, school, and organization has the skills, spaces, and systems to make rehabilitation and inclusion a normal part of how they operate.

Our Values

How we work

  • 01

    Dignity first

    Every person we serve, directly or through the teams we train, is treated with respect and full personhood.

  • 02

    Capacity, not dependency

    We transfer skills and systems so our partners can sustain the work without us.

  • 03

    Practice informed by evidence

    Our training and consulting draw on sound occupational therapy and rehabilitation principles.

  • 04

    Partnership over transaction

    We embed into the organizations we work with rather than delivering an isolated service.
